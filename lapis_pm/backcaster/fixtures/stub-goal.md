# Goal

What would need to exist for Zephyrium to support friend-group-shaped
collaboration without enterprise scaffolding?

A friend-group-shaped collaboration space is one where:
- The group is small (2-12 people who know each other)
- No enterprise admin, IT department, or org-tier account required
- Members can contribute creative work and retain ownership
- The group can form, collaborate, and dissolve without platform lock-in
- Participation costs are person-scale, not org-scale

This goal-state is the DoD worked example for Backcaster v0.
